        Method

        Every trophy shows its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them, taken from the official 55-trophy list as Exophase and PSNProfiles listed it before this guide was written (the two agree on every name, description and grade). Every guide beside them is written for this entry, in our own words, and cites the published guides its facts come from. A source is cited, never copied.

        Where the published guides disagree or say a method is still being confirmed, this guide says so instead of inventing a step: five trophies (Challenging Tank, Personal Best, Bad Luck, I Guess, The Truth Is Out There and Ten Pin) carry that honest flag. Counts (42 Shooting Range challenges, 14 drone race locations, 18 meditation spots, 100 WAiNK locations, 52 areas of Leonida, 9 gangs, 8 garage properties) come from the official list and the cited guides.

        No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork, box art or screenshot of the game appears anywhere in this guide: the look is our own, drawn in code.
